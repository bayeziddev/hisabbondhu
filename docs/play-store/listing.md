## Full description — English
```
HisabBondhu — your shop's accounts, always at hand.

A simple Bangla digital ledger (khata) for small shops and businesses in Bangladesh. Record sales, dues (baki), payments, expenses, purchases and stock — even without internet.

✅ Works offline
Every entry is saved on your phone first. When you're online it backs up and syncs automatically — even if the app is closed.

✅ Credit ledger (baki khata)
• See what each customer and supplier owes at a glance
• Credit sale, payment received, payment given — recorded in 10 seconds
• Collection-date reminders
• Polite Bangla messages via WhatsApp, SMS, Messenger, IMO
• Share PDF account statements

✅ Sales, purchases & expenses
• Cash and credit sales, sales returns
• Credit purchases, supplier payments
• Expense categories like rent, salary, electricity
• Separate totals for Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank and card

✅ Stock
• Stock updates automatically with every sale and purchase
• Low-stock alerts and stock value
• Pieces, kg, litre, dozen, carton and more

✅ Reports
• Today's sales, cash collected, credit sales, expenses and estimated profit
• Daily, weekly and monthly reports
• Export to Excel (CSV) and full backup file

✅ Accurate and trustworthy
Money is calculated to the paisa. Mistakes are cancelled with a reversal instead of being deleted, so your history stays clean.

✅ Your data is yours
Login is optional. No ads, we never sell your data, and you can export or permanently delete it at any time.

Great for grocery shops, general stores, pharmacies, restaurants, fashion, mobile & electronics, wholesale and service businesses.

Questions or ideas? Use “Contact us” inside the app.
```
